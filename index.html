Three things FSAA adds

01

The graduation pattern

Feature UI lives flat in features/<name>/ui/. It is promoted to shared/ui only after a second feature actually reuses it.

02

The boundary test

One question decides shared vs feature: could the component be described without business vocabulary? Generic DataTable passes; projectColumns does not.

03

No widgets layer

Large compositions are features, not a UI level. Container/presentational split happens at the export level, not in the filesystem.

Positioning

Position

“FSAA is not a new architecture theory. It is a practical, opinionated assembly of two proven methods — Feature-Sliced Design and Atomic Design — with the missing decision rules added. We do not claim a revolution.”
